How to play

  1. Take turns placing a number from 1 to 5, or an ×, on any empty square.
  2. A number can't repeat in its row or column. An × can go anywhere.
  3. When a row or column is full, count how many numbers it holds. Whoever owns the piece showing that count wins the line. If no piece shows it, nobody does.
  4. A line is settled early once no legal move can change its result. The bar on its edge fills in: red, blue, or hatched for nobody.
  5. On their first turn, the second player may steal the opening move instead of playing.
  6. When all ten lines are settled, whoever won more lines wins.

Keys: arrows to move, 1–5 or X to place, U to undo, S to steal, Esc to clear.
